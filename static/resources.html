Christianity Resources

If you're curious about Christianity or interested in learning more about the faith, here are a few resources I would recommend! Whether you're just beginning to explore who Jesus is or looking to grow deeper in your faith, I hope you find something helpful here.

Explore Christianity

  1. Two Ways to Live A visual explanation of the gospel and the Christian message.
  2. The Chosen (TV series) A series depicting the life and ministry of Jesus.
  3. Alpha A welcoming, in-person space to explore questions about life, faith, and Christianity through conversations with others. Let me know if you'd like to check out an Alpha course in NYC. I'd be happy to come with you!

Read and Understand the Bible

  1. The Bible Read the Bible online or explore different Bible translations.
  2. Bible Reading Plans Guided reading plans to help you read Scripture regularly and explore different topics and books of the Bible.
  3. BibleProject Animated videos exploring the books, themes, and overarching story of the Bible.
  4. BibleProject Classroom Free, in-depth courses for those who want to study Scripture more deeply.
  5. Got Questions Answers to common questions about Christianity, theology, and the Bible.
  6. The Gospel Coalition Articles, podcasts, and resources on biblical teaching and Christian living.
