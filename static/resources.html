Read and Understand the Bible

  1. The Bible You can read the Bible online or through apps such as YouVersion. If you are reading the Bible for the first time, I recommend the New Living Translation (NLT) and beginning with the Gospel of Mark. You can then proceed to the other Gospels (Matthew, Luke, and John), followed by Acts and Romans, before continuing with the rest of the New Testament. If you prefer a more formal translation, you might also enjoy the English Standard Version (ESV).
  2. BibleProject Animated video overviews of each book of the New Testament.
  3. Read the Bible in One Year A guided reading plan to help you engage with Scripture regularly, accompanied by videos to deepen your understanding. You can explore other reading plans here.
  4. Gospel of Mark Bible Study Course A free, in-depth Bible study course exploring the historical and cultural significance of Jesus as the Christ, His life and ministry, the meaning of His death and resurrection, and what it means to follow Him.
  5. Got Questions Answers to common questions about Christianity and the Bible.

Commentaries

If you come across a passage you don't understand, turn to God first, for He is the author of Scripture. Ask Him for wisdom and understanding. Bible commentaries and study resources can also be helpful, but remember that they are supplementary tools, not substitutes for reading Scripture yourself.
