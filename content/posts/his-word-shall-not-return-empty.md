Today, I went to the morning prayer set at Church of the City. I arrived around 6:45 AM, so I was able to join the latter part of the 6 AM prayer set before staying for the next one at 7 AM. Today, we meditated on Isaiah 55, and as we contended for the city of New York in prayer, I wrote down this prayer in my notebook.

**Isaiah 55:11**

“So shall my word be that goes out from my mouth; it shall not return to me empty, but it shall accomplish that which I purpose, and shall succeed in the thing for which I sent it.”

**Lord Jesus,**

Your Word commands us to go and make disciples of all nations. Yes, O Lord, we believe that Your Word will not return to You empty. You have revealed to us that a day will come when a great multitude that no one could number, from every tribe, people, and language, will stand before Your throne and before the Lamb. I look forward to that day, O God!

Thank You, Father, for choosing to use unworthy servants like us to advance Your Kingdom. I pray that You would help us to obey You faithfully. Would You equip us with boldness and courage, O God? Not by might, nor by power, but by Your Spirit.

Show us, O God, where You are already at work and how we can partner with You here in New York City. Please increase my sensitivity to Your Spirit. And would You purify my motives, O God? Put to death whatever remains of myself that stands in the way of Your will. Decrease me, O God. I want You to receive all the glory, honor, and power, for You and You alone are worthy!
