Today, as I was listening to the book of Acts and making my way through chapter 17, it dawned on me that the name *Berean* (as in the Berean Standard Bible, or even [The Berean Test](https://www.thebereantest.com/), a website I occasionally use to evaluate gospel songs) actually refers to the people of Berea, mentioned in verses 10–11.

> Now these Jews were more noble than those in Thessalonica; they received the word with all eagerness, examining the Scriptures daily to see if these things were so.”

It was just a random thought that crossed my mind, but it also served as a reminder of the kind of believer I aspire to become: someone who, like the Bereans, diligently searches the Scriptures to discern whether what is being taught aligns with God's Word, rather than blindly accepting it. After all, Scripture is our ultimate authority.

May I become more like a Berean!

On a separate note, I've been using the [Public Reading of Scripture (PRS) app](https://prs.app/bible/gen.1.nlt) to listen to the Bible, and I thought I'd share it here! It's free, allows you to download audio for offline listening, and supports continuous chapter-by-chapter playback, so you don't have to manually navigate to the next chapter.
