Today, as I was listening to the book of Acts and making my way through chapter 17, it dawned on me that the name *Berean* (as in the Berean Standard Bible, or even <a href="https://www.thebereantest.com/">the Berean Test website</a> that I occasionally use to help me evaluate gospel songs) actually refers to the people of Berea, who were mentioned in verses 10–11.

> “Now these Jews were more noble than those in Thessalonica; they received the word with all eagerness, examining the Scriptures daily to see if these things were so.” (Acts 17:11)

This was just a random thought that crossed my mind, but I came to understand why the name *Berean* is often associated with critically examining whether what we hear aligns with Scripture, rather than simply accepting what is fed to us at face value.

After all, just because something, let's say a song, is labelled as a gospel song, does that necessarily mean its lyrics are biblical and its message glorifies God?

I believe that every Christian ought to aspire to become more like the Bereans, diligently searching the Scriptures to discern whether what we are taught is true. May we always turn to Scripture as our ultimate authority.

May I become more like a Berean!
