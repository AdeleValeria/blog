Happy Thanksgiving from Baltimore!

Since last year, I have had the pleasure of spending Thanksgiving with a beloved friend named Ruby. I woke up at 4 AM this morning and made my way from New York City to Baltimore by bus.

On this year's menu: carrots with cilantro chimichurri sauce, mashed potatoes, stir-fried tofu with peanut sauce, garlic kale, jollof rice, roasted lamb, and all sorts of desserts, including upside-down pear gingerbread cake and homemade sweet potato pie.

As usual, we went around the table and shared what we were most grateful for this year. For me, it was the grace of God through Christ's sacrifice.

There were things that happened this year that I would consider personal failures. And while I could feel shame and guilt knocking at my door over all the ways I had been humbled this year, these experiences ultimately led me to depend all the more on God's mercy and grace rather than give in to self-condemnation. God has been teaching me that His grace is sufficient, and that I can simply rest in Him and trust in His goodness.

And, of course, second on my list of things I am most grateful for is my family and friends. Looking around me, I cannot help but feel grateful for the people God has so graciously placed in my life: my mom, my sister, my brothers and sisters in Christ in Baltimore, and now also in NYC, where I live. God has been so, so good, y'all!
