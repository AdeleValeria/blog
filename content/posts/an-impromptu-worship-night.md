My Graduate Christian Fellowship friends in Baltimore and I got to have an impromptu worship night during my visit! What started as a simple plan to grab ice cream at R. House turned into such a sweet, sweet evening of worshipping the Lord together in a cozy living room.

I am so encouraged to see my friends earnestly seeking after God. They mentioned that one of the things they had missed since I left Baltimore was our worship nights, and it made my heart so full that we got to share that time together again during my brief visit, even though it was entirely unplanned. What a gift it is to have friendships centered on Christ!

Truly, there is no better way to spend a Friday night, especially during this Thanksgiving season, than worshipping God together. And how wonderful it is to look forward to the day when we will gather before His throne and worship Him for all eternity. Thank You, Lord Jesus!
