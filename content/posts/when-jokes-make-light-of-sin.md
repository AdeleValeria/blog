Today, I was chatting with a friend about jokes that might not seem harmful on the surface but make light of things that Scripture calls us to turn away from.

It started when I jokingly said, "It's written in Matthew 29:28 that bla bla bla." Of course, Matthew 29:28 doesn't exist in the Bible, haha! My friend was quick to remind me of **Revelation 22:18–19**, which warns us against adding to or taking away from the words of Scripture.

This also reminded me of something that happened a few months ago. I was teasing a good male friend of mine about how another male friend had apparently thought he was hitting on him when he offered to cook for him and host him at his place so they could spend some time talking about faith. My friend and I found the misunderstanding amusing, and we continued joking about it throughout that week.

But later, God convicted me through **Ephesians 5:3** that it was not fitting for me to make light of something that Scripture calls sin, especially when sin is what Jesus went to the cross to bear the penalty for.

> But sexual immorality and all impurity or covetousness must not even be named among you, as is proper among saints.

My prayer is that even in our jokes, we would not become so casual about sin that we find amusement in the very things that grieve God's heart. May God continue to cultivate in us a deeper reverence for His holiness.
