Today, I went out for evangelism again with a few other people in Brooklyn. One of the highlights was having the opportunity to encourage a fellow sister in Christ and pray with her.

I asked her how confident she would be that she would go to heaven if she were to die today. At first, she wasn't sure, although I could tell that she was a genuine believer with a sincere desire to grow in her relationship with God. I was grateful for the opportunity to remind her that the sacrifice Jesus made on the cross is more than enough. She shared that she sometimes felt she needed to do more, whether that meant reading the Bible more consistently, serving more, or doing other things to earn God's love. Her busy schedule as a student made her feel like she was falling behind in doing all these things, and that guilt somehow made her doubt whether God was still for her.

I reminded her that neither height nor depth can separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus! His love for us is so deep that we cannot fully comprehend it. I also shared how Satan, the father of lies, wants us to doubt God's love for us. Praise God that she seemed encouraged.

We also talked about abiding in God and not being too hard on ourselves on days when we miss our usual Bible-reading routine. I shared how important it is to keep looking to Jesus, just as Peter did, even after his failure. What's most important is that we keep running back to God rather than allowing ourselves to believe that we have gone too far to return to Him. That, too, is a lie Satan wants us to believe.

May God continue to draw my sister in Christ closer and closer to Him!

All in all, it was a fruitful evening. We ended up staying an hour and a half longer than planned, finishing at 9:30 PM. I joined around 6 PM, while a few others had started as early as 4:30 PM. It is exciting to see what God is doing in this neighborhood!
