What's been on my mind today is starting a letter club! I've been seeing artists all over the internet sending monthly letters to their subscribers, usually including a handwritten or printed note and little goodies like stickers, postcards, and other stationery. Since I'm so into letter writing myself, I thought, why not try something similar?

I'm still brainstorming how I could use this as a way to share the Good News. I'd also want to make it completely free, with no cost involved for anyone receiving the letters. Another thing I'm trying to figure out is the frequency. Should it be a one-off letter (like an introductory packet about the Gospel), a monthly letter, or perhaps both? And what could I include in the letters as someone who isn't an artist? If anyone has ideas or suggestions, I'd love to hear them!
