Today's post is a little different from my usual ones. I'm excited to announce a new page on my website: a little restaurant </a href="https://adelevaleria.com/splitter">bill splitter</a>!

I certainly vibe-coded the whole thing, but man, the joke is original (wink).

I just wanted to create something simple that each diner can quickly access. Last time, my friend introduced me to this app called Tab to split our restaurant bill, and I thought it would be nice to have a straightforward, web-based tool. Instead of relying on one person to input the price of all the items for the app to compute, why not just have everyone get their phones out and enter what they ordered themselves? That way, there's no need to pass one phone around. No account creation, no app download, and no unnecessary AI integration.

Please give it a try, and let me know if you find any bugs!
