Today’s post is a little different from my usual ones. I wanted to share something I built over the past hour: a restaurant <a href="https://adelevaleria.com/splitter">bill splitter</a>! I certainly vibe-coded the whole thing, but the joke is certified original (wink).

Last time, my friend introduced me to this app called Tab to split our restaurant bill, and I thought it would be nice to have a straightforward, web-based alternative. Instead of relying on one person to input the prices of everyone's orders, why not have everyone get their phones out and enter what they ordered themselves? That way, there's no need to pass one phone around. No account creation, no app download, and no unnecessary AI integration.

I made this for my own personal use, but who knows, you might find it useful too!
