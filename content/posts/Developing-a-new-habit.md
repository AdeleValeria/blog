# I Will Write Every Day

I usually share my life updates and reflections on social media, but I’m hoping to transition out of them. I am not joking when I say that my Instagram posts and stories are already essay-like.

I’ve always loved writing, not only whatever happens to be on my mind, but also things like letter writing and snail mail! I think slowing down and reflecting by writing down my thoughts helps me be a little more intentional about how I live.

And I’m giving myself a very ambitious goal for this blog: to write every day. Some days, it might be a longer post. Other days, it might be nothing more than a sentence or two. Let’s give it a try, shall we?
