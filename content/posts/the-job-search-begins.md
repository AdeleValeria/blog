Today, I officially started job hunting, especially since I expect to receive my Employment Authorization Document sometime in December or January. I updated my resume and applied to a few jobs on LinkedIn.

For now, my main focus is on computational biology and bioinformatics, since those are the areas in which I have the most educational training and research experience. However, I am also interested in technical education, technical customer-facing roles, and responsible AI. I think I would do well in these areas, too. I enjoy working with people, so I have been thinking about what kinds of positions would allow me to leverage both my technical background and interpersonal skills. Having spent most of my time in academia, I am still learning about the emerging roles and career paths available beyond academia.

I am still limiting my search to New York City or remote roles. I have found a home in the church communities here, and my faith has been greatly encouraged by seeing street evangelists boldly preach the gospel and intercessors earnestly contend for this city. Because of this, I have been asking God to allow me to stay in New York a little while longer. At the same time, I am very open to being redirected and sent somewhere else if that is where He leads me. I do have faith that He will make a way for me to stay in NYC, though!

I don't know what kind of role I will end up getting, but I have faith that it will be a job that fills my cup. I trust that it will be more than just a means of paying my bills. I genuinely see work as an act of worship, and I want to approach it as something I do for the Lord and for His glory. I also hope to be intentional about building meaningful relationships with my coworkers and being a good representative of Christ in my workplace.

And who knows? Perhaps there might even be opportunities to start a prayer gathering or Bible study, or to support one that is already happening there. I have learned that many companies in NYC, including some of the big Wall Street firms, have prayer gatherings organized by Christian employees. I think that is wonderful, and I would love to be part of something like that. I trust that God will honor this desire and lead me to a workplace where I can grow both professionally and as a follower of Jesus, contribute meaningfully, and flourish.
